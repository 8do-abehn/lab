I'm a systems administrator and aspiring DevOps engineer learning by building.

## What I'm Learning

- **Infrastructure as Code**: Ansible for configuration management across my Proxmox cluster
- **Virtualization**: Proxmox VE, GPU passthrough, LXC containers
- **Networking**: Tailscale, DNS, VLANs, PXE boot
- **Windows Server**: Active Directory labs, Group Policy, enterprise administration
- **Containers & Orchestration**: Docker, Kubernetes, Helm

## The Lab

My homelab runs on a Proxmox cluster with mixed hardware - repurposed workstations, GPUs for passthrough, and Ceph storage. It's a playground for learning enterprise patterns at home scale.

## Why I Write

Documenting what I learn forces me to understand it better. If something here helps you, even better.
